According to the Koran, nowehere does Allah promise Ibrahim that, in Ismail, shall his seed be called, 2) Isaac is pinpointed to be born after the trial: 'later'. It follows that As-Saffat 102 refers to Ismail.
Ibrahim had a dream, did not know, beforehand, that the victim, Ismail, should survive, and went thereupon performing the sacrifice without demur.

Contrariwise, the Bible states that Abram/Abraham was first promised, while in awake state, not in a dream, that his seed, as the stars of heaven, would be in Isaac, and only then, "after these things", was he tested.

Abraham is praised for his faith for he believed the Genesis 15:5 and Genesis 21:12 promises: since, at the time the temptation occurred, Isaac had no descendant yet, Abraham knew, owing to this his faith in his God, that Isaac wouldn't perish.

Clearly, Allah is not the god of Abraham and the latter isn't Ibrahim.
Both gods and men of strikingly irreconcilable personalities.
